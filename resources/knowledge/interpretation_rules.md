# Trade Analytics Business Interpretation Rules

## Business Rules for Trade Dynamics
1. **Surplus & Deficit Context**:
   - A trade surplus where exports exceed imports suggests competitive manufacturing or strong external demand.
   - A trade deficit requires examining whether imported capital goods (machinery, technology) are driving long-term productivity.

2. **Concentration Risk**:
   - If top 3 partner countries account for more than 70% of total export value, highlight as geographical concentration risk.
   - Product sector dependency: a single product group commanding >50% of trade denotes structural vulnerability.

3. **Data Quality Governance**:
   - Upstream validation issues (e.g., duplicate entries, zero or negative values) must be explicitly flagged on the audit page.
   - Multi-currency mixtures without exchange rate policies invalidate monetary aggregates.
